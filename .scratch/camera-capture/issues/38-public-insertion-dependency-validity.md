## Current behavior

Split from 35 before source changes. Read 35 and 34 Comments and resolved authorization 29. Scene::insertChildNode and direct public ForestNode insertion leave both SceneIdentity revisions unchanged; transform/object helpers explicitly signal. Forest constructs its root through make_ForestNode, before a Layer constructor body can install a hook. ObjectPool::emplace builds temporaries; append/reserve can relocate existing nodes; reused and chosen slots use move assignment. These are not editable payload move-out operations.

Scene::layers() exposes shared Layer pointers. Layer stores raw Scene ownership; LayerNodeData stores raw Layer ownership and resolves objects through it, including in its destructor. Scene::~Scene clears delegates, resets defaults, clears its LayerMap, then clears object pools. Clearing the map does not destroy retained Layers. Detached copied object payloads can outlive a Layer even while the Scene lives. A raw Scene epoch pointer, or a guard solely on Scene lifetime, does not qualify this ownership chain.
